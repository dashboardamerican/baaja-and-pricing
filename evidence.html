5. Existing obligations are part of the counterfactual

No tariff named AND does not mean all incremental infrastructure payments would be new. AEP reports SB Energy's commitment to fund $4.2bn of Piketon transmission. Wisconsin's PSC identifies $1.099bn of Oracle-funded E-STATCOM equipment. Entergy's SEC filing describes incremental-cost recovery from Meta through contributions in aid of construction, advance payments and minimum bills. These arrangements require project-specific reconciliation before estimating additional policy receipts.

Evidence and source links. These examples do not establish a national prior-recovery share, and bundled utility savings claims cannot be deducted dollar-for-dollar from a transmission estimate.

7. Correct the comparator and preserve the distinction between the two outputs

For inherited requirement B0, annual incremental cost I and new-load billing share s:

Embedded payment = s × (B0 + I)
Higher-of payment = max[embedded payment, I]
Credited AND payment = s × B0 + I
Additional vs embedded = (1 − s) × I
Additional vs higher-of = min[(1 − s) × I, s × B0]
Above incremental cost = s × B0

Both cases collect the same total grid requirement. Additional receipts equal other-customer bill relief under full crediting. Above-incremental payments and additional payments overlap; do not add them. With embedded pricing, additional receipts can exceed the amount above incremental cost because the baseline may shift some incremental cost to others. The calculator now defaults to embedded and retains higher-of under Details.

For policy scale, compare additional receipts to the ITC illustration. JCT's historical official score is $11.279bn nominal over FY2022–2031. Repeating its normalized ten-year profile gives $31.7bn over 30 years; retaining the initial profile then holding its final annual level for 20 more years gives about $53.4bn. Both are analyst extrapolations, not official 30-year scores or estimates of guaranteed ratepayer savings. Private bill transfers are not federal budget offsets.
