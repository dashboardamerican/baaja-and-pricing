CAPITAL CALIBRATION AUDIT — 25 OCTOBER 2026

Recommendation
Retain $0.65m/MW only as a judgmental GROSS full-network planning assumption. The independent primary records make this a plausible central engineering scenario, not a statistically estimated national mean. Use $0.3m-$1.0m/MW as a deliberately broad engineering sensitivity. Do not label $0.65m/MW “newly assignable after existing funding.” That description is contradicted by documented large customer-funded projects. A national prior-funding fraction is not identified by the available records; any central fraction must be explicitly judgmental.

If the requested outcome is total AND payments ABOVE INCREMENTAL CAUSAL TRANSMISSION COST, then under correctly credited AND the amount is the ordinary inherited-network contribution. The new-capital assumption cancels. It matters critically for additional payments compared with existing tariffs, or for payments above fully allocated embedded cost. Do not silently switch between those outcomes to manufacture a defensible number.

What this supports
Costs are geographically heterogeneous and complete network packages reasonably can exceed local-connection estimates. A rounded$0.65m gross scenario is defensible as explicit expert judgment because it lies above observed local packages and below the largeEvest conditional fullpackage; it is not defensible to say the data estimate$0.65m nationally. No observed national residual$perMWcan be produced from these records without an assumption about regional shares, cost causation, and prior funding. The $22bn OPC headline cannot fix this: it aggregates procurement windows with different futureload/retirementbases and disputed allocation, not paired marginalDCMWor actual payments.
