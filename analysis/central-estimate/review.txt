DOLLAR AND TIME CONVENTIONS
Real levelized central recovery is internally consistent as a financing proxy; it is not a filed utility revenue requirement or actual cash billing profile. The synthetic nominal rate-base alternative converts vintage nominal recovery back to real dollars consistently, but omits utility-specific tax/deferred-tax and financing details as disclosed. The horizon is 2027–2056, with policy receipts only in 2030–2056 (27 charged calendar years); say '30-year window' rather than implying 30 full years of charges. No terminal recovery after 2056 is counted. Capital enters in its cohort year with full-year recovery and no delay or retirement before the 40-year life—an explicit coarse annual approximation.

The central $46.81bn specifically sums modeled real levelized payment equivalents within the window. Inflation-indexing those equivalents produces the $75.44bn nominal scenario; it does not convert them into observed or approved annual utility cash receipts. The nominal rate-base recovery sensitivity is a separate synthetic cash-timing approximation, yielding approximately $52.60bn in constant dollars. Therefore describe the headline as 'modeled additional payments using levelized cost recovery' and document that convention, rather than calling it an audited cash-flow score.
