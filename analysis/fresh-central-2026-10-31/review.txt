Two wording fixes recommended before delivery:
1. In the one-pager, replace "Existing customers’ direct funding is assumed to cover 35% of capital recovery" with "Existing direct-funding obligations are assumed to cover 35% of the modeled data centers’ capital recovery". Current wording can imply incumbent customers fund the new customers’ capital, whereas D represents the modeled data centers’ obligations that exist without BAAJA.
2. In calculate.py headline_definitions.extra_above_baseline (and regenerated JSON), replace "AND less embedded transmission payment plus common preexisting direct obligations" with "AND less the sum of embedded transmission charges and common preexisting direct obligations". Parentheses/sum are essential because the current prose can be read as P1-E+D instead of P1-(E+D).

Optional clarity: describe PJM rates and billing pools as "actual starting PJM zonal rates and billing pools" because future inherited loads and recovery are projected. The about-$55bn headlines are reasonable coarse rounding of $56.0bn and $55.6bn, both disclosed immediately below.
